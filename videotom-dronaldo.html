VIDEOTOM x DRONALDO (Hengermalom Kelesztés)

VIDEOTOM x DRONALDO (Hengermalom Kelesztés)

DESCRIPTION

Created for the Hengermalom – Kelesztés program, this audiovisual performance was part of the first public activation of the former Budai Hengermalom site, a newly emerging cultural and community space in Budapest. The project, developed as Dronaldo × Videotom, was built around a room-sized analogue TV installation using more than twenty CRT televisions as its visual foundation. The video signals were processed and distorted through modular systems and other analogue tools, while the live soundscape was performed with synthesizers, pedals, and various electronic devices. The video presented here is a test demo version of the performance, offering an impression of the atmosphere, visual language, and technical setup of the installation.
