Brenda – Te Falta La Ene’

Brenda – Te Falta La Ene’
DESCRIPTION

“Te Falta La Ene’” is a music video created for Brenda, a Colombian producer and artist. The project marked my first larger-scale shoot as a director, developed in close collaboration with Brenda from the early concept phase. We began shaping the visual world in September 2024 through moodboards, sketches, and an animatic. After the pre-production phase, the video was shot over two days in December at two distinct locations: Napsugár ABC and Lósi Major. The project brings together live-action directing, editing, VFX, and animation into one cohesive audiovisual piece.
