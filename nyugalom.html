Nyugalom (Video)

NYUGALOM

DESCRIPTION

“Nyugalom” is a short video etude created for Noémi Fábián’s Self-Representation class at MOME. The project explores the idea of rest and tranquility in a fast-paced world, combining contemplative visuals with a quiet, reflective atmosphere.
