OAKEN X BORU

DESCRIPTION

Oaken × Boru is a collaborative audiovisual project built around a special musical material created for the 2026 Fekete Zaj Festival. The project focuses on the musical and visual realization of this collaboration, centered on a full-length album recorded this year and accompanied by a filmed visual album session shot at the historic Őskohó in Újmassa. The film combines the performance of the material with a carefully designed visual environment shaped by the atmosphere and architectural character of the location. Its production involved an extensive planning process, including 3D animatics in Blender, technical previsualization, and a pre-programmed lighting setup synchronized with the music. The final film is planned to be released in early 2027.
