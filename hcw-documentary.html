HCW documentary (Short documentary)

HCW documentary

DESCRIPTION

A short documentary made for the MOME Video Report class, filmed during Véres November, a live Hungarian pro wrestling event by Hungarian Championship Wrestling. Shot over the course of one day, the film combines interviews with participants, wrestlers, and audience members with observational moments from the atmosphere of the live show. The project explores the performative, physical, and community-driven world of Hungarian amateur wrestling.
